Privacy notice

What we do with what you send us.

Last updated 24 November 2026

What this notice covers

This notice explains how information sent to YLSWAN through this website, or by email to info@ylswan.shop, is handled. It covers the website only — not the records we keep about suppliers, distributors or employees.

What we collect

  • The name, email address, enquiry type and message you enter in the contact form.
  • Anything you choose to include when you write to us by email.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends to any web server, such as the page requested and the time of the request.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry, to follow up on a commercial conversation you started, and to keep a record of that correspondence. We do not sell contact data, and we do not add people to a marketing list because they sent a one-off enquiry.

How long we keep it

Enquiry correspondence is kept for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and any resulting commercial relationship, then removed in the ordinary course of record keeping.

Who can see it

Only the people who need to answer you. We use service providers for email and web hosting, and their handling of data is governed by their own terms.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics script. Fonts are loaded from a public font service, which may record the request in the usual way any web request is recorded.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete your enquiry. Write to info@ylswan.shop and we will act on the request.

Contact

Questions about this notice: info@ylswan.shop
